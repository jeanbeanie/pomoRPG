# PomoRPG

## Have you done your part in the war against procrastination?

PomoRPG is a simple pomodoro tool inspired by Role Playing Games (RPGs). 
Set a timer dedicated to completing the project of your choosing without distraction. 
While you work, your loyal adventurer will be battling monsters and completing quests in the background.
Staying focused rewards you with experience points (XP), which can be used to level up skill trees.

## How to Use
1. Choose a project or task you want to work on.
1. Set a pomodoro timer (default is 25 minutes) and start working on your task without distractions.
While you work, your adventurer will be battling monsters and completing quests in the background.
1. When the timer goes off, take a break and check your adventure log to see how your adventurer did.
Your adventurer earns experience points (XP) after each pomodoro cycle.
1. Every 100 XP earns you a skill point, which can be used to level up your skill tree in one of three branches: 
	- Consistency - build streaks and show up for yourself daily
	- Focus - be rewarded for achieving flow state
	- Sustainability - build endurance and prevent burnout
1. Every 4 pomodoro cycles triggers a boss battle, earning even more bonus XP and rewards.
1. Repeat the process, striving to build consistency and focus over time.
1. **Hardcore mode:** For an extra challenge, try playing in hardcore mode where you can only take a strict 5 minute break after each pomodoro cycle, and pausing the timer is not allowed.
Completing hardcore mode successfully counts as a "clean run" and earns you bonuses, and a special badge in your adventure log.
